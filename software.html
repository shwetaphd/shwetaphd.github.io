frohmod

frohmod performs a mode-resolved analysis of Fröhlich (polar-optical phonon) scattering using the output of a standard VASP phonon calculation, by finite differences or DFPT, together with the Born effective charges and the high-frequency dielectric tensor. It applies the direction-dependent non-analytical correction at Γ to obtain the LO–TO splitting and the LO eigenmodes along any chosen direction. For each LO mode it evaluates the frequency, the Fröhlich coupling strength and its thermal weight at a given temperature, which identifies the modes that dominate polar-optical scattering, and it resolves each mode into elemental contributions. The displacements of any selected mode can be exported as V_Sim animations and VESTA files, and the results are written as data tables and publication-ready plots.
